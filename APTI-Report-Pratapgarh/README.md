# APTI Screening Report — Pratapgarh, Uttar Pradesh

A 15-page project report on **Air Pollution Tolerance Index (APTI) based screening of
sensitive and tolerant plant species at various locations in Pratapgarh district,
Uttar Pradesh** (study area: Bela Pratapgarh and surrounding locations).

## Contents

| File / folder | Description |
|---|---|
| `APTI_Screening_Pratapgarh_UP.docx` | The main 15-page Word report (title page, certificate, abstract, 7 chapters, 2 photo plates, references). |
| `images/` | Real photographs of the study area and the 12 screened species, location maps, and data charts used in the report. |
| `data/compiled_apti_literature.csv` | The compiled APTI dataset (species × published study) underlying Tables 5.1–5.2 and the charts. |

## Report structure

1. Introduction
2. Review of Literature
3. Study Area and Sampling Locations (S1–S7)
4. Materials and Methods (APTI formula, leaf-parameter protocols, classification)
5. Results (compiled APTI evidence, photo plates, charts, location-wise screening)
6. Discussion
7. Conclusion and Recommendations
8. References and photo credits

## Sampling locations

- **S1** City core — Paltan Bazaar / Railway Road, Bela Pratapgarh
- **S2** Pratapgarh Junction–Chilbila corridor on NH-330
- **S3** Raniganj–Patti road (SH-128) junction
- **S4** Sukhpal Nagar industrial estate & brick-kiln cluster
- **S5** Kunda town market
- **S6** Patti town market
- **S7** Dahilamau–Shukulpur aonla belt (GI cluster, rural control)

## Data basis

All data are taken from published sources: peer-reviewed APTI studies from comparable
Indo-Gangetic cities (Prayagraj, Lucknow, Noida, Delhi, Muzaffarpur, Udaipur, Dehradun,
Varanasi), CPCB/UPPCB air-quality reports, the Census of India 2011, the KVK Pratapgarh
district profile, the Geographical Indications Journal (Pratapgarh Aonla), and published
air-quality dashboards (IQAir, aqi.in). Photographs are real images taken from internet
sources and are credited on the last page of the report.

The report is a literature-based screening; a seasonal field-validation protocol for
Pratapgarh is proposed in Chapter 4.

## Using the report

Open the `.docx` in Microsoft Word or LibreOffice Writer. Fill in the name, roll number,
course and institution on the title page and in the certificate/declaration before
submission. Page numbers, tables and figures are already laid out for 15 pages.
